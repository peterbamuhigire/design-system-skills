# Hospitality, hotel and restaurant Kaizen - 2026-09-14

## Change

Added a hospitality UX overlay for guest websites and staff operations. It
requires one visual thesis, purposeful type/palette choices, real content,
critical service states, accessible status signals, low-bandwidth testing and
rendered handoff. It distinguishes editorial guest surfaces from dense,
scan-friendly front-desk, housekeeping, kitchen and cashier screens.

## Evidence discipline

The books contributed durable principles about service flow, restaurant design,
front-office coordination, events and human hospitality. No book was treated as
authority for current fonts, accessibility law, platform behaviour or property
facts. The design doctrine and current primary sources remain controlling.

## Experiment and gate

Hypothesis: designing guest and staff journeys together reduces conversion and
operational friction. Measure task completion, state-discovery errors,
accessibility defects and handoff rework on a real rendered slice. Rollback by
unlinking the overlay. Next review: 2026-10-14.
