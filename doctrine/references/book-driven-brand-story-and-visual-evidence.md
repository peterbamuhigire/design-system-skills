# Book-driven brand story and visual evidence

This reference is an independent synthesis of durable principles from the supplied brand, archetype, storytelling, product-marketing, launch, instructional-design, and improvement books.

## Meaning before styling

Define audience, context, promise, proof, desired action, boundaries, and voice before selecting colour, type, imagery, layout, or motion. Use archetypes as a disciplined qualitative probe, never as demographic or behavioural evidence.

## Coherent evidence

Treat every touchpoint as part of one experience. Establish narrative hierarchy, show the human problem and change, and map important claims to visible proof and a source owner. Visual polish cannot compensate for a weak offer, unclear decision, inaccessible interaction, or unsupported claim.

## Learning and iteration

Prototype the smallest useful change, test comprehension and behaviour, inspect accessibility and performance, and document what becomes a standard. Separate reaction to an artefact from learning, transfer, and outcome evidence.

## Currentness and accessibility gate

Current accessibility requirements, browser or platform behaviour, security or privacy claims, and tooling guidance must be verified through Digital Research. For this wave, use the current WCAG 2.2 source and the portfolio currentness register; the design doctrine remains the authority for typeface bans, visual QA, and anti-slop controls.

Before release, check semantic structure, keyboard access, focus, contrast, text resizing, reduced motion, responsive states, content accuracy, and source freshness.
