# Uncertainty and Evidence Message Design

This reference is a self-contained synthesis prepared from Sara Rubinelli,
*Institutional Health Communication in the Information Age*, and Rana Gujral,
*The AI Instinct*.

## Output structure

For consequential AI or health information, show the claim, evidence or source
role, known limits, uncertainty, date or freshness, safe next action, and human
or professional route. Use plain language without stripping conditions that
change interpretation. Invite correction and show how the system will respond.

Avoid percentage confidence badges that imply calibrated certainty without
evidence. Design empty, stale, conflicting, inaccessible, and corrected states;
the user must be able to distinguish no evidence from evidence that disagrees.
