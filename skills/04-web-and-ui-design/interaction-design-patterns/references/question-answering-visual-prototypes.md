# Question-Answering Visual Prototypes

Prototype one unresolved interaction or hierarchy question at the lowest fidelity that preserves the
decision. State the question, hypotheses, decision owner, participants/evidence, observation, threshold,
and disposal path before making the artefact.

Produce structurally distinct variants. Change navigation, hierarchy, grouping, interaction, density,
or task sequence—not only colour, type, or decoration. Use realistic content and include the critical
normal, empty, error, loading, permission, narrow-width, keyboard, and reduced-motion states needed to
answer the question.

Do not connect a throwaway prototype to production credentials, customer data, payments, or
irreversible actions. After evaluation, delete or quarantine the prototype, or rewrite the selected
behaviour under production architecture and design-system controls. Visual polish does not promote it.

Evidence includes the predeclared question, variant differences, rendered captures, accessibility and
failure checks, observations, decision, limitations, and cleanup result. Missing participants, render,
browser, or device evidence is `NOT ASSESSED`.

This reference adapts the prototype-as-question mechanism studied in Matt Pocock's
`mattpocock/skills` repository at commit `3cca18b` and applies the design engine's originality and
anti-slop controls.
