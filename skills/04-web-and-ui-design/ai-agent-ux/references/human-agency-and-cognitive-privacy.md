# Human Agency and Cognitive Privacy

This reference is a self-contained synthesis prepared from Rana Gujral,
*The AI Instinct: The Future of AI and Human Decision-Making*. It turns the
book's human-agency argument into interface requirements without treating its
speculative future scenarios as product facts.

## Agency requirements

Make clear what the system observed, inferred, recommended, changed, and still
needs from the person. Provide inspect, correct, refuse, pause, stop, undo,
escalate, and exit controls at the moment of consequence. Do not make assistance
feel like an irreversible transfer of judgement.

## Cognitive privacy checks

Show what memory or behavioural signal is being used, why it matters, how long it
persists, and how the user can edit or delete it. Avoid affective or behavioural
inferences that are unnecessary for the task. Separate confidence in a model
output from authority to act, and test whether the interface pressures agreement.
