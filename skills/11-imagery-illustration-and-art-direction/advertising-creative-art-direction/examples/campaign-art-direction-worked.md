# Worked example: art direction for a mobile-money savings campaign

Fictional client used to show the method: "Tereka", a Ugandan mobile-money savings wallet. No
results are claimed; the campaign has not run.

## 1. Brief restated (from the marketing engine's brief packet)

| Field | Restatement |
|---|---|
| Objective | Grow first-time savers; communication goal: "saving small amounts daily is normal and safe" |
| Audience | Market vendors and boda-boda riders in Kampala and Mbarara, 22-40, paid daily in cash or mobile money |
| Current perception | "Saving is for people with salaries; small amounts are pointless" |
| Single message | Save from UGX 500 a day and watch it grow |
| Proof | Regulated wallet partner and interest terms (evidence held by the marketing engine's legal gate) |
| Call to action | Dial the USSD code or open the app |
| Languages | Luganda, Runyankore, English |
| Placements | Taxi-rear stickers, market posters, Facebook and Instagram feed and Stories, WhatsApp status |

## 2. Construction choice

Mixed-language, mixed-literacy audience: **image-led** with a short headline in each language.

## 3. Concepts and screen

| Concept | Key visual | Headline (Luganda version translated) | Cover tests | Score | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A "Coins to jerrycan" | A vendor's small daily coin stack growing, frame by frame, into a new water jerrycan and then a market stall | "UGX 500 today. Your stall tomorrow." | Image alone shows growth; headline adds amount and time: pass | 6 | Advance |
| B "Rider's receipt" | A boda rider's daily route drawn as a line that ends in a school-fees receipt | "Every trip, a little saved." | Pass; strong for riders, weaker for vendors | 6 | Advance |
| C "Piggy bank" | A pink piggy bank on white | "Start saving today!" | Image and headline say the same thing; imported symbol | 2 | Killed |

Responsible-creative checks on A and B: swap test passed; people shown as capable earners, not
as needing rescue; no party colours; the partner's claim wording held for legal review.

## 4. Big-idea test pack

- One sentence: "Small daily amounts become visible progress."
- Three-channel sketch: taxi sticker (coin stack only), 15-second Story (stack grows), market
  poster (full sequence with USSD code). Works in all three: pass.
- Press-release test: "Kampala vendors turn loose change into savings" can be written honestly
  if the scheme launches as described: pass, conditional on launch facts.
- Forward test: the final frame plus two lines survives as a WhatsApp status image: pass.

## 5. Campaign system

Chosen: **triplets** for taxi stickers, posters and prospecting social (high frequency, short
exposure). Master template: coin-stack visual lower left, headline upper left, USSD code in the
brand yellow panel lower right, logo bottom edge. Only the end object (jerrycan, stall, fees
receipt) and the headline change. Locked assets: brand yellow, the coin-stack motif, the
wordmark, the three-note audio sting used in video.

## 7. Hand-back packet

Concept boards A and B with scores; triplet system sheet; layouts for the two verified
placements; the open specification checks; alt-text briefs and subtitles plan; rights notes for
the commissioned local photography; the critique record showing the client chose A with B's rider
variant as a triplet member for riders.
