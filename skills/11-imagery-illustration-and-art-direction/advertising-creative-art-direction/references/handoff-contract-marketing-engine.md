# Hand-off Contract with the Marketing Engine

Parent skill: [`../SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md) (`advertising-creative-art-direction`).

**When to read:** at the start of any advertising job that comes from the social-media and
digital marketing engine (`social-media-skills`, resolved through the global engine routing
table), and again at hand-back. The marketing engine owns strategy, audience, offer, copy, media,
legal release and measurement. This engine owns visual concept, art direction, layout and
production specification. Neither engine duplicates the other's doctrine.

---

## 1. What comes in (brief packet)

| Field | Owner | Minimum content |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign objective and measure | Marketing | One business objective, one communication objective, the metric and date |
| Audience and mindset | Marketing | Named segment, current perceptions, what they must think or do after exposure |
| Single message and proof | Marketing | One takeaway; reasons to believe with evidence sources |
| Big idea or platform line | Marketing | One sentence, if already agreed; otherwise marked "to be developed jointly" |
| Offer and call to action | Marketing | Price, terms, destination URL or WhatsApp number |
| Approved copy or headline options | Marketing (copy) | Real words, in each language needed |
| Channels and placements | Marketing (media) | Placement list with dates and budgets per placement |
| Mandatories and prohibitions | Marketing and client | Legal lines, disclosures, regulated claims, colours or symbols to avoid |
| Brand assets | Client | Logo files, colour builds, typefaces with licences, imagery library, sound |
| Decision-maker and review dates | Account lead | One named approver; review calendar |

If the message is not single-minded, the audience is "everyone", or proof is missing for a claim
the visuals must carry, return the packet with questions. Do not start concepts.

## 2. What goes back (design packet)

| Deliverable | Content | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Concept boards | At least three distinct concepts, each with key visual, headline in place, construction type and a one-line effectiveness score and reason | Poster and cover tests recorded |
| Campaign art-direction system | Triplet or cousin rules, locked assets, imagery treatment, type and colour roles | Do and do-not examples |
| Placement layouts | One layout per placement, built to the current specification | Specification source and date per placement |
| Production files | Final artwork in the required formats, named by campaign, placement, version and date | Preflight or export check passed |
| Accessibility evidence | Captions and subtitles plan, alt-text briefs, contrast checks for text on images | Checks listed per unit |
| Rights and provenance notes | Photography, illustration, music, talent and font licences; AI provenance where used | Rights register entries |
| Critique and decision record | Feedback translated to brief goals; chosen and rejected concepts with reasons | Signed by the named decision-maker |

## 3. Ownership of decisions

| Decision | Owner | This engine's role |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy, audience, offer, message, budget | Marketing engine and client | Challenge if the brief blocks good visual work |
| Big idea wording | Marketing engine (with creative input) | Run the big-idea test pack and report |
| Visual concept, construction, art direction, layout | This engine | Own and defend with reasons |
| Copy wording | Marketing engine (copy) | Flag length or redundancy with the image |
| Claims, disclosures, regulated content | Marketing engine legal and market release gate | Never waive; mark visuals conditional where evidence is pending |
| Release of finished assets | Marketing engine creative review gate | Supply the design evidence packet |
| Measurement and optimisation | Marketing engine | Supply variant files and naming for tests |

## 4. Iteration loop

1. Marketing sends the brief packet.
2. This engine returns questions within the agreed turnaround, or starts concepts.
3. Internal concept screen (7-point scale, responsible-creative checks).
4. Joint review with the named decision-maker; decision recorded.
5. Layouts and production files built to current specifications.
6. Marketing runs its creative review and legal gates; returns fixes with the brief goal each fix
   serves.
7. This engine revises and re-renders; the marketing engine releases.
