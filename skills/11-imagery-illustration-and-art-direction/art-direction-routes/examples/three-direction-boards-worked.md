# Worked example: three direction boards for a private clinic group

Fictional client used to show the method: "Bukoto Family Clinics", three outpatient clinics
in Kampala. Budget allows one local photo shoot. The clinic's marketing officer edits the website
herself after launch.

## Pre-filter

| Item | Record |
|---|---|
| Audience | Salaried Kampala parents, 28-45, choosing a clinic for children and themselves |
| Primary job | Check services and prices, book or call, find the nearest clinic |
| Stigma to overcome | "Private clinics are expensive and intimidating" |
| Mobile job map | Call or WhatsApp; directions by landmark; see prices; book a slot |
| Device reality | Mid-range Android on mobile data for most visitors (confirm with analytics after launch) |
| Category audit | Competitors use navy and white, stock photographs of smiling doctors, auto-rotating sliders |
| Hard gates | WCAG 2.2 AA; editable by one non-designer; light hero on mobile |

## Board A - Safe fit: R1 Superclean Authority with warm human imagery

- **Thesis:** For parents choosing care quickly, a calm, orderly site signals competence and
  fair dealing because prices and booking are visible on the first screen; it avoids the cold,
  navy "hospital" look of the category.
- **Dials:** style superclean; theme none; intensity n/a.
- **Type character:** institutional serif headings over a humanist sans body (faces chosen in
  `font-selection-and-pairing`).
- **Palette logic:** warm off-white base, deep teal primary to stand apart from category navy,
  one coral accent for booking only.
- **Imagery rule:** real clinic staff with real patients (with consent), daylight, eye level.
- **Hero mock:** "Children's check-ups from UGX 40,000. Book today, or call us." with the
  Ntinda clinic's real reception photograph.
- **Mobile view:** price line, "Book" and "WhatsApp us" buttons, nearest clinic by landmark.
- **Cost tiers:** build Low; maintain Low (all text live, one image slot per page).
- **Risks:** can feel plain. Mitigation: one signature detail (a hand-drawn clinic map).
- **Currency check:** no dated treatments.

## Board B - Stretch: R4 Illustrated World

- **Thesis:** For anxious parents, a commissioned illustration world makes the clinic feel
  human and child-friendly because children appear as confident characters, not patients; it
  avoids stock-photo cliché.
- **Dials:** style clean; theme illustrated neighbourhood; intensity styled conventional.
- **Imagery rule:** commissioned illustration system with a small character set; photography of
  real staff on the team page only.
- **Cost tiers:** build Med-High (illustrator commission); maintain Med (new pages need new
  spot art or reuse of a library).
- **Risks:** may read as childish to adult patients. Mitigation: illustration limited to the
  children's services section and empty states.
- **Currency check:** no dated treatments.

## Board C - Bold: R6 Tactile Craft at subtle intensity plus R3 type-led headlines

- **Thesis:** For parents who distrust glossy marketing, big plain-spoken headlines and honest
  close-up photography of real care signal "no hidden costs" because the site talks like a
  trusted nurse; it avoids polished corporate gloss.
- **Dials:** style editorial; theme real material and hands (close-ups of care); intensity subtle.
- **Cost tiers:** build Med; maintain Low-Med (headlines live; photographs refreshed yearly).
- **Risks:** large type needs disciplined copy. Mitigation: headline bank written with the
  content team.
- **Currency check:** an auto-rotating hero slider was proposed by the client; classed dated and
  replaced with one fixed hero and a services grid.

## Decision criteria agreed before the presentation

On brand; stands out from category navy; readable and fast on a mid-range phone; editable by
the marketing officer; trustworthy to first-time visitors.

## Decision recorded

The client chose Board A, with Board C's plain-spoken headline style carried across as an agreed
borrow. Board B was rejected because the maintenance tier exceeded the marketing officer's
capacity. The decision register lists the criteria, the reasons and the parked illustration idea
for a possible children's campaign later.

No claim is made about booking or conversion results; those will be measured after launch.
