# Data-Foundation to Decision Visualisation

This reference is a self-contained synthesis prepared from Sanjeev Mohan,
*Designing the AI-Driven Data Foundations: Architecture, Principles, and
Practice*. It connects data-product lineage to a visual decision surface.

## Decision exhibit contract

Name the decision, audience, data product, grain, freshness, owner, and source
lineage. Show missingness, uncertainty, comparison basis, and the consequence of
acting on stale or low-quality data. Keep a drilldown from summary to the
underlying record or evidence.

For AI-generated or agent-mediated exhibits, expose the model or rule version,
data cut, human review state, and correction path. A visually clean chart does
not repair an undefined metric, broken reconciliation, or unauthorised audience.
