# Worked Example: From Brief to Infographic Specification

This is an illustrative example. The figures are invented for the exercise and must not be reused as facts.

## Brief

- Audience: a small-business owner deciding whether to adopt a weekly cash review.
- Decision: decide whether a simple weekly routine is worth testing.
- Medium: one-page A4 handout and a 1080px social adaptation.
- Consequence of failure: the reader remembers a slogan but cannot perform the routine.

## Visual thesis

**A cash review is a short route from three signals to one decision, not a full accounting lecture.**

## Storyboard

1. **Title:** “Three checks before you commit next week’s cash.”
2. **Orientation:** a clear route marker shows the reader moving from bank balance, expected receipts, and committed payments to a go/hold decision.
3. **Main exhibit:** a three-stage horizontal path with one measured card per signal; each card uses a number placeholder, unit, period, and question.
4. **Reveal:** a decision gate shows `GO`, `HOLD`, or `VERIFY`, using text plus shape, not colour alone.
5. **Action:** a four-line weekly routine with owner, day, evidence, and escalation.
6. **Source note:** “Illustrative workflow; replace with the organisation’s approved definitions and records.”

## Form and metaphor

- Form: process diagram with a small comparison table, not a pseudo-dashboard.
- Metaphor: a route with checkpoints; it reinforces sequence without pretending that cash is physically flowing along a pipe.
- Illustration rule: one restrained hand-drawn arrow family; no coins, rockets, gauges, or decorative 3-D money.

## Visual decisions

| Element | Choice | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Focal point | Decision gate is largest and appears after the three checks | Makes the action, not the decoration, memorable |
| Type | Editorial display face for the title plus a licensed readable humanist for labels | Gives the handout an authored voice while preserving small-size clarity |
| Colour | Warm paper base, charcoal text, one deep teal accent, orange only for “verify” | Calm, high-contrast, and not dependent on red/green semantics |
| Density | One idea per checkpoint and generous gutters | Supports scanning and a printable version |
| Labels | Direct labels beside each checkpoint | Eliminates legend matching |

## Validation

- Data: all values are placeholders and visibly labelled illustrative.
- Scale: no quantity is encoded by decorative coin size.
- Accessibility: status is written and shaped; contrast and text alternative are required checks.
- Responsive: social version becomes four vertical frames; it is not a shrunk A4 poster.
- Failure path: if one input is missing, the route terminates at `VERIFY` rather than implying `GO`.
- Human read: reviewer must explain the first action after a five-second glance and the full routine after reading once.
