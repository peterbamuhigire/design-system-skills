# Worked example: two print jobs for a fictional Kampala client

Illustrative only. "Rwenzori Agro Co-operative Union" is a fictional client; the printer answers
below are examples of what to record, not facts about any real printer.

## Job A - A4 roll-fold leaflet (six panels), 5,000 copies

### Questions asked and answers recorded (dated at the time of asking)

| Question | Answer recorded | Consequence for artwork |
|---|---|---|
| Process? | Sheet-fed litho, four colour | Build in CMYK; no RGB |
| Profile and ink limit? | Printer supplied its coated profile and a total ink limit for the chosen stock | Separations converted to that profile; coverage checked in preflight |
| Stock? | 150 gsm matt coated, locally held | Crease before folding: yes (printer advises) |
| Panel widths? | Printer supplied widths with the fold-in panel narrower | Artwork built on the printer's template, not three equal thirds |
| Bleed and safety? | 3 mm bleed, 5 mm safety | Live text kept 5 mm from trim and from each fold |
| Rich black and overprint? | Printer's rich black recipe for the back-panel solid; black text overprints | Large solid uses the recipe; small text 100 per cent K only |
| Proof? | Digital contract proof, then a printed sample on the stock | Client signs both |

### Decisions

- The brand green was specified as a Pantone in the brand guide. The job is four colour and the
  budget does not allow a fifth plate, so the green was converted to the printer's CMYK build and
  shown to the client on a printed swatch before approval. The brand guide now records the CMYK
  build for this printer.
- The fold-in panel carries the order form; its safety margin was widened so the form's rules do
  not fall into the fold.

## Job B - 48-page annual report, saddle stitched, 800 copies

| Item | Setting | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Trim | A4 portrait | Confirmed in writing with the client |
| Cover | 250 gsm silk, soft-touch matt lamination on the outside | Tactile cover; lamination tested on a sample first |
| Text | 115 gsm silk | Printer confirmed 48 pages saddle stitch on this stock |
| Creep | Printer applies creep in imposition; designer keeps centre-page text 8 mm from outer trim | Centre pages lose outer margin after trimming |
| Gutter | No faces or detailed images across the centre spread | Staples and fold distort the gutter |
| Finishes | Spot varnish on the cover photograph only | One finish serves the idea; foil declined after the printer said it would be subcontracted with a longer lead time |
| Proof | Contract proof of cover and four inner spreads; paper dummy of the full extent | Client handled the dummy to approve weight and feel |
| Press check | Cover only; account lead attends with the signed proof | Brand colour and photograph are the risk items |
| Contingency | Three working days | Rainy-season humidity can slow drying |

## Preflight record (excerpt)

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Fonts embedded | Pass |
| Images at about 300 ppi effective | Pass after replacing two web images supplied by the client |
| Unused spot colours | Fail at first run: an old Pantone swatch remained; deleted, re-run, pass |
| Total ink | Pass after reducing shadows in one dark photograph |
| Black text overprint | Pass |
| PDF standard | As requested by the printer; recorded in the specification |

## Outcome record

The specification sheet, signed proofs, preflight report and press-check note are stored with the
final files. Delivered quantity and trim were checked against the specification on receipt.
No claim is made here about print quality beyond what the signed proof and press check record.
