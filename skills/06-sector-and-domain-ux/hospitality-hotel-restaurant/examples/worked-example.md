# Worked Example: Lodge Booking Flow

## Brief

The property needs a booking journey that communicates trust and works on a
low-cost mobile device. The visual thesis is “quiet confidence with a clear
path to arrival.”

## Authored decisions

- Pair an approved expressive display face with a readable UI/body face after
  the font licence and local availability checks.
- Use restrained nature-led colour only for brand expression; reserve semantic
  colour plus text/icons for availability, payment and safety states.
- Put verified room facts, inclusions, cancellation/deposit terms, location and
  human contact beside the conversion action.

## Proof required

Render the room page and booking states on mobile and desktop; test keyboard
focus, contrast, readable labels, slow loading, invalid dates, sold-out state,
payment failure, confirmation and human fallback. Record reviewer, defects,
retest and owner decision. Until those artefacts exist, the flow is
`NOT_ASSESSED`.
