# Child and Learning Experience Contract

Return to [the parent skill](../SKILL.md).

## Cohort record

Record age/developmental context, language/literacy, prior knowledge, abilities/access settings, devices/input, solo/guardian/teacher context, session conditions and exclusions. Age alone is not a complete cohort.

## Learning record

State the construct, observable performance, core verb, prior knowledge, scaffold sequence, feedback, independent/transfer task, comparator or baseline, measure, duration, accountable education owner and claim limit. Engagement, completion, enjoyment and retention are separate outcomes.

## Safety and stopping

Cover pause/save/resume, clear session endings, stopping reminders where appropriate, no streak loss/confirm-shaming/nagging, safe failure, guardian controls, social/contact boundaries, reporting/moderation, data minimisation, deletion, incident escalation and accessible alternatives.

## Commercial gate

Ads, purchases, targeting, profiling, random rewards and third-party SDKs fail closed until age, territory, platform, privacy, legal, content and guardian requirements are approved. Permitted ads are clearly labelled and non-intrusive, never obscure controls, never interrupt play/learning/narrative/recovery/stopping/exit, have neutral decline/close and tested no-fill/offline/error/kill-switch paths. Do not make declining a rewarded ad punitive.

## Evidence

Use approved child-research safeguards. Separate observation from interpretation. Record comprehension, independent use, learning measure, enjoyment, frustration, accessibility, wellbeing/stopping and guardian/teacher utility separately, with limitations and adverse events.
