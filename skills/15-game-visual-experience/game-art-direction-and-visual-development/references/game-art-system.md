# Game Art System

Return to [the parent skill](../SKILL.md).

## Visual-pillar record

For each pillar record: player feeling/verb; observable shape/value/colour/material/motion choice; where it applies; where it must not apply; gameplay-readability contribution; cultural/rights note; production and performance cost; representative proof; disconfirmation signal.

## Asset-bible minimum

Include world scale and camera, silhouettes and proportion, shape language, palette roles and colour script, value/lighting/material rules, surface/texture finish, characters/factions, environments/landmarks, props/affordances, VFX priority, UI-world seam, animation posture, 2D/3D cohesion, asset ladder/LOD, names/source files/exports, rights/provenance, localisation/store crops, accessibility, budgets, review and rejection examples.

## VFX priority

Reserve the strongest value contrast, saturation, area, motion and audio-visual synchrony for the highest gameplay consequence. Cosmetic ambience yields to threats, player actions, objectives and failure/recovery cues. Provide reduced-motion, flash-safe and non-colour-only alternatives.

## Cultural control

Research, inspiration, consultation, permission and rights are separate fields. A motif with unresolved meaning, community authority, sacred/restricted status, living-person rights or commercial permission remains quarantined. Name the accountable reviewer and preserve disagreement.
