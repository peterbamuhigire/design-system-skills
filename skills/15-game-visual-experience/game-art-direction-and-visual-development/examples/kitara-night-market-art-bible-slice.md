# Worked example: Kitara night-market art-bible slice

Evidence level: documented fictional example. It does not establish historical fact, community permission, cultural approval, asset performance or player readability.

| Pillar | Observable rule | Boundary/test |
|---|---|---|
| Guided night warmth | Navigable stalls use warm clustered light against quiet cool paths | Navigation must still work in high-contrast and colour-adjusted modes |
| Readable craft silhouettes | Interactable objects have one strong outer profile and uncluttered grip/contact area | Check at gameplay camera, not concept-art zoom |
| Living, not museum-still | Repeated cloth, smoke and crowd rhythms imply current activity | Motion intensity is adjustable and never masks hazards |

Meaning-bearing royal, clan, sacred or historical motifs remain neutral placeholders until the fact/fiction ledger, named cultural review, commercial permission and rights decision exist. The asset ladder requires blockout, representative hero/ordinary prop, far-distance simplification, low-spec target render and source/export record. VFX priority is player action → hazard → objective → ambience.
