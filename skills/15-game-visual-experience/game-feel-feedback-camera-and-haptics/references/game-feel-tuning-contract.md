# Game Feel Tuning Contract

Return to [the parent skill](../SKILL.md).

## Verb phase row

Record: phase; player intent; authoritative state; target response; animation/pose; VFX; audio; UI; camera; time effect; haptic; intensity tier; reduced/off alternative; budget; cancellation/recovery; build/device evidence; player finding.

## Channel priority

Allocate the strongest channel combination to the most consequential event. Input acknowledgement must not look like confirmed success. Camera and time effects never conceal required information or remove player control. Haptics are semantic and never load-bearing.

## Parameter record

Avoid “snappy,” “epic,” or “subtle” without numbers. Record input-to-visible response, phase durations, easing/spring, amplitude, frequency, spatial falloff, camera axes, shake envelope, zoom/FOV, hit-stop/time scale, particles/overdraw, audio onset/ducking and haptic event. Values remain hypotheses until measured in the identified build.

## Evidence

Use before/after build IDs and change one related parameter set at a time. Device evidence covers latency, frame pacing, thermal/repeated exposure and platform haptics. Player evidence separately covers comprehension, control, comfort, satisfaction and fairness. Preserve negative and mixed results.
